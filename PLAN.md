## Summary

Plain-language explainers and a documented guide to exploring the public NASA Exoplanet Archive, for classrooms and curious learners.

## Scope

In scope: explainer articles, a beginner's guide to the archive's key fields, and worked example queries. Out of scope: hosting live services or redistributing the raw archive.

## Risk

Low: built on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; educational/derivative in nature, no personal data, and no farm-/case-specific prescriptive advice. Each deliverable must cite sources and stay within established, verifiable material.

## Approach

Each task produces one self-contained Markdown explainer or guide with sources and a CC-BY header.

## Definition of shipped

Classroom-ready explainers and a documented data guide published openly and usable in a lesson or outreach setting.
